The problem

Most finance tools explain the past. Arbor is built to help you prepare for the future.

Traditional tracking stops at transactions, balances and spending history. Arbor connects those records to the questions behind real financial decisions:

  • How financially resilient am I?
  • How much debt am I really carrying, and how fast is it falling?
  • How many months could my savings cover if income stopped?
  • How does my net worth change as loans, assets and cash move?
Features

One system for every part of your money.

Household finance and full business accounting in one platform. Every entry updates the numbers instantly. No spreadsheets, no formulas to maintain.

₹

Accounts and ledger

Bank accounts, cash and wallets with opening balances, a searchable ledger, and net worth calculated as of today.

Assets and loans

Investments, property and vehicles grow or depreciate at their own rates. Loans with EMI schedules pay down automatically on a reducing balance.

Household Financial Resilience Index · implemented

Measuring more than wealth.

The question isn't just "how much money do I have?" It is "how well can my financial position withstand change?" Arbor answers it with the HFRI, an independent research framework by Vivaan Agarwal, using the same published scoring model as the research.

HFRI = 0.25·S + 0.25·D + 0.25·E + 0.25·X
S · 25%

Savings Strength

Savings rate (Y − C) ÷ Y. Can the household create a surplus?

D · 25%

Debt Sustainability

40% debt-to-income, 60% debt-service-to-income. How heavy are loans and EMIs?

E · 25%

Emergency Coverage

Liquid savings ÷ monthly essential spending. How many months are covered?

X · 25%

Expense Stability

Coefficient of variation of monthly spending. How predictable is it?
